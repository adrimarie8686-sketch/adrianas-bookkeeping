Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Have another question? Contact us and we’ll be happy to discuss your business needs.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Adriana’s Bookkeeping welcomes businesses across industries, including service providers, contractors, retailers, independent professionals, online businesses, startups and growing companies.

Do you work with clients outside Bakersfield?

Yes. We serve clients in Bakersfield, California and offer virtual bookkeeping support to businesses nationwide, subject to service fit and availability.

Do you offer payroll services?

Payroll and employee-record services can include payroll processing support, timekeeping records, earnings records, payroll calculations and payroll reporting. The exact scope, software responsibilities, filings and deadlines will be agreed upon in writing before service begins.

Do I need to use QuickBooks?

Not necessarily. We can discuss your current accounting software and whether QuickBooks setup or optimization would be appropriate for your business.

How is my monthly price determined?

Pricing depends on transaction volume, number of bank and credit card accounts, payroll frequency and employee count, inventory complexity, number of entities or locations, and the level of reporting and communication required.

Can you clean up past-due bookkeeping?

Yes. Historical cleanup projects are quoted after reviewing the period involved, the condition of the records and the amount of work needed.

How do we get started?

Schedule an introductory consultation. We’ll discuss your business, current bookkeeping process, goals and the support you need. If we’re a good fit, you’ll receive a written proposal and service agreement.

Do you provide tax, audit or legal services?

Bookkeeping support and financial reports are not a substitute for tax, audit, legal or investment advice. We can coordinate with your CPA or tax professional. Any regulated services must be provided by appropriately qualified professionals.
